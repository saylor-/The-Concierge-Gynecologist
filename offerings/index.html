Offerings

Focused care for the transitions that matter

From the first signs of perimenopause to life after cancer treatment, every concern is met with time, evidence, and a physician who knows your history.

  • Perimenopause

    Recognizing the early shifts (irregular cycles, disrupted sleep, mood and memory changes) and building a plan before they compound.

  • Menopause

    Individualized, evidence-based care for hot flashes, sleep, bone, heart, and genitourinary health, including hormone therapy when it’s right for you.

  • Sexual function

    Thoughtful evaluation of low desire, arousal concerns, and painful intercourse, addressed with the time and privacy they deserve.

  • Cancer survivorship

    Gynecologic care after cancer treatment: early menopause, vaginal and sexual health changes, and non-hormonal options when hormones aren’t advised.

  • Previvor surveillance

    Coordinated screening and risk-reduction planning for women carrying BRCA or other inherited mutations who have not had cancer.

  • Abnormal uterine bleeding

    Careful evaluation of heavy, irregular, or postmenopausal bleeding, with clear explanations and the full range of treatment options.

  • Pelvic pain

    A patient, trauma-informed approach to chronic or cyclical pelvic pain, including endometriosis and pain with intercourse.

  • Vulvar conditions

    Diagnosis and treatment of lichen sclerosus, chronic itching or irritation, vulvodynia, and other vulvar skin concerns.

  • Endometrial biopsy

    A brief in-office sampling of the uterine lining to evaluate abnormal or postmenopausal bleeding, with comfort measures throughout.

  • IUD insertion

    Placement and removal of hormonal and copper IUDs for contraception or bleeding control, with attention to your comfort.

  • Colposcopy & LEEP

    Follow-up for abnormal Pap or HPV results: a magnified cervical exam and, when needed, in-office removal of precancerous cells.

  • Hysteroscopy

    A camera-guided look inside the uterus to evaluate bleeding, polyps, or fibroids, often with treatment in the same visit.

Care model

Gynecologic care, inside the practice you already trust

Dr. Harrington provides gynecologic care, consultations, and select procedural services on-site at partnering concierge primary care practices across the Denver metro area.

Image: practice setting

A partnership

Not a separate practice

Rather than an independent practice, this is a partnership model. Dr. Harrington works within primary care offices that already provide concierge care to their patients.

You’re seen in a setting you already know, with the same unhurried, personal attention you expect from your concierge physician.

How it works

Three simple pieces

  1. 01

    Your practice partners with Dr. Harrington

    Concierge primary care offices bring her in as their gynecologic partner.

  2. 02

    You’re seen on-site

    Consultations, gynecologic care, and select procedures take place at your practice.

  3. 03

    Your care stays connected

    Gynecologic care sits alongside the primary care relationship you already have.

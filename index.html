Meet your physician

Lauren Harrington, MD

A Stanford-trained, board-certified OB/GYN and Menopause Society Certified Practitioner, Dr. Harrington specializes in the nuanced care of midlife transitions: perimenopause, menopause, and beyond.

For over a decade, she has been empowering women through every stage of their health, pairing evidence-based medicine with warmth and personal connection.
